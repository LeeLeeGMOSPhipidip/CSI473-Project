Only users with a valid University of Botswana (UB) email address may register on the system
Users are assigned roles such as Student (customer/Owner) and Administrator
Only registered Owners may create item listings
An item can only be rented if its available quantity is greater than zero.
A rental is only considered valid once both Customer and Owner confirm payment.
All rentals must have a defined return date.
Only completed rental transactions may be rated.
Administrators may generate reports on user activity, rentals, and disputes.
